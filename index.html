2. Overview (in short)

Start Force One is an app for analysing track-and-field starts and finish times. All data you record stays exclusively on your own device. The app runs no servers of its own, transmits no data to us or to third parties, and contains no advertising or analytics/tracking libraries.

The only network feature is the direct connection between two of your own devices (the start camera and the finish camera) over Wi-Fi Direct, used to transfer a recorded finish video. In the process the data never leaves the circle of your own devices.

4. Where is the data stored?

All data is stored exclusively locally on your device (the app's internal database and device storage). There is no storage on the provider's servers or in any cloud. We, as the provider, have no access at any time to your data.

In addition, the app's "Backup" feature lets you create and restore a local backup archive of your data. Where you keep that archive (device storage, an external card, a cloud folder of your choosing) is solely your responsibility.

5. Data transmission / recipients

  • No transmission to the provider or third parties. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services and contacts no external servers.
  • Device-to-device transfer (two-camera mode): When you connect two of your own devices, the recorded finish video is transferred directly between those devices over a Wi-Fi Direct connection. That radio link is WPA2-encrypted by the Android system, and the connection is additionally protected by a one-time connection token. The transfer happens only between your devices; no provider server and no third party is involved.

7. No third-party processing / no tracking

The app uses no advertising IDs, no crash/usage tracking and no cloud AI. The pose/motion detection (MediaPipe) runs entirely offline on the device; no image or personal data leaves the device in the process.

8. Retention and deletion

You determine the retention period yourself. Recordings and analyses remain stored until you delete them in the app. Uninstalling the app removes the app's internal data from the device; backup archives and exported files you created yourself may need to be deleted separately.

9. Recordings of other people (club/youth context)

If athletes — especially minors — are recorded, you as the user (e.g. coach/club) are responsible for obtaining the necessary consent from the data subjects or their legal guardians in advance and for complying with applicable data protection law. Since all data remains locally on your device, you are also responsible for storing and deleting it securely.

10. Children

The app is aimed at coaches and athletes, not at children as independent users. Where data of minors is recorded, Section 9 applies.

11. Your rights

Because we, as the provider, do not collect or store any of your data, we cannot provide information about, hand over or delete your specific content — full control rests with you on the device. Where a responsibility exists, you have the rights under the GDPR to access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; in Austria this is the Austrian Data Protection Authority (DSB), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna (dsb.gv.at).
